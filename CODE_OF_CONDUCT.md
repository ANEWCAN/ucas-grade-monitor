# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, respectful feedback, accepting
responsibility, and focusing on what benefits the community. Unacceptable
behavior includes harassment, insults, sexualized attention, doxxing, and other
conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional
setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior through the repository's private security or
maintainer contact channel. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments,
commits, code, issues, and contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.

This document is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1.
